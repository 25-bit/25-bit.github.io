ACADEMIC RESEARCH WEBSITE

1. Open index.html in a browser to preview the website.
2. Replace "Your Name", university, email, research details, publications, etc.
3. Put your CV in this folder and name it cv.pdf.
4. Put your photograph in this folder as profile.jpg.
5. Replace the # links with your Google Scholar, ORCID, LinkedIn, DOI, etc.
6. To add publications, copy one <div class="pub">...</div> block.
7. To add research topics, copy one <article class="card">...</article> block.

The site is responsive and works on desktop and mobile.
