About me

A little about my work

My research focuses on hybrid quantum systems, with particular emphasis on cavity magnonics. I work on the theoretical investigation of hybrid light–matter systems, exploring the interaction between microwave photons, magnons, and phonons in cavity-magnonic platforms.

My research aims to understand and control nonlinear, nonequilibrium, and quantum phenomena in these systems, including quantum correlations, nonlinear dynamics, bistability, quantum interference, and magnon–photon interactions. By developing theoretical models and numerical approaches, I investigate how cavity-magnonic systems can be engineered to realize and manipulate quantum states and phenomena. A central goal of my research is to explore the potential of cavity magnonics as a platform for quantum technologies, with possible applications in quantum information processing, quantum communication and the generation and control of nonclassical states.
